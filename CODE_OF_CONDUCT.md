# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct / 贡献者公约行为准则

## Our Pledge / 我们的承诺

In the interest of fostering an open and welcoming environment, we as contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in our project and our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

为了营造开放、友善的环境，我们作为贡献者与维护者承诺：无论年龄、体型、残障、族裔、性别特征、性别认同与表达、经验水平、教育背景、社会经济地位、国籍、外貌、种族、宗教或性取向，都让我们的项目与社区成为一个没有骚扰的参与体验。

## Our Standards / 我们的标准

Positive behaviors include: using welcoming and inclusive language; being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences; gracefully accepting constructive criticism; focusing on what is best for the community; showing empathy toward other community members.

正向行为包括：使用友好包容的语言；尊重不同观点与经验；虚心接受建设性批评；以社区利益为先；对其他成员展现同理心。

Unacceptable behaviors include: sexualized language or imagery and unwelcome sexual attention; trolling, insulting/derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks; public or private harassment; publishing others' private information without explicit permission; other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

不可接受的行为包括：性化语言或图像及不受欢迎的性关注；挑衅、侮辱/贬损性评论及人身或政治攻击；公开或私下骚扰；未经明确许可公开他人隐私信息；其他在专业场合可被合理视为不当的行为。

## Enforcement Responsibilities / 执行职责

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards and will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behavior they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

项目维护者负责澄清并执行本准则，并对任何被认为不当、威胁性、冒犯或有害的行为采取适当且公正的纠正措施。

## Scope / 适用范围

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces and when an individual is representing the project or its community.

本行为准则适用于所有项目空间，以及个人代表项目或社区时的场合。

## Enforcement / 执行

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the maintainers. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.

滥用、骚扰或其他不可接受行为可向维护者报告。所有投诉都将被及时、公正地审查与调查。
