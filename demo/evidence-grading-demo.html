Evidence Grading for Verifier Decisions

Separating evidence quality from authorization validity — three worked cases, machine-runnable, externally verified.

Mechanism Three moving parts

1 · Evidence grading axis

Each claim is assigned a grade by how it was obtained and how checkable it is — never by who the actor claims to be.

V · VerifiedS · StrongM · Moderate L · LimitedP · Pending

2 · Authorization validity, separate

A second dimension states whether the presented authorization is valid, out of scope, expired, or unverifiable — evaluated independently of evidence quality.

3 · Explicit failure classes

Outcomes distinguish MUST-reject cases, distinguishable failure classes, and log-and-proceed boundaries, so verifiers fail in the same observable way.
